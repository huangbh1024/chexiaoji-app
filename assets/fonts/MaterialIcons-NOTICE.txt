Material Icons
==============

This font is used only for the vector icons in exported PDF reports
(vehicle strip, month-over-month trend, verification footer).
It is a glyph subset (12 icons) of the Material Icons font bundled with
Flutter, distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
